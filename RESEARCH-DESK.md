# Investo Research Desk publishing

The library and articles are static pages, not an automatic publishing service.

1. Prepare a dated article. Credit the actual author or editorial team; obtain their review before publication. Do not invent an individual author, qualifications or approval.
2. Use the layout in post-stock-analysis-methodology.html. Update title, canonical URL, description, social metadata, visible dates and Article structured data together. Keep the original publication date when updating a note and show the update date separately.
3. Add primary-source references next to material facts. Record source dates and distinguish evidence, assumptions and opinion. Do not reuse dated prices as current commentary.
4. Confirm applicable role, conflicts and risk disclosures. This educational section does not confer regulated analyst/adviser status. Security-specific recommendations need an appropriate authorised publishing process.
5. Add a static card to insights.html with data-rd-category and data-rd-search, and add the URL to sitemap.xml. Categories: market, methodology, sectors, newsletters, education. Newest first.
6. For a monthly letter, create newsletter-month-year.html and add the edition to the visible newsletter archive. An edition dated mid-month must not imply a complete month-end report.
7. Check links, search/filter, mobile layout, metadata and sources. Publish only approved material. There is no email-list storage or automated WhatsApp subscription.

Suggested editorial rhythm, subject to actual review and approval: market commentary when material developments warrant; methodology and sector explainers as reviewed; one curated newsletter edition per month. No future publication is promised or scheduled by this implementation.
